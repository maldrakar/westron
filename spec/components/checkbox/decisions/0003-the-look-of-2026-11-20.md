# 0003. The look of 2026-11-20

Date: 2026-11-20. Status: the quiet ring is the owner's choice, made on prototype renders; that the accepted contrast
deviation covers the ring, and that high contrast keeps the strong ring, are the coordinator's assumptions, recorded
here **awaiting the owner's confirmation** at the review of the snapshot matrix (decisions/0005, the second gate). It
amends the approved contract, which stays approved: the description and `ring.color` of `checkbox.spec.yaml`, and the
choice of `border.strong` for the ring in decisions/0001.

## Context

With the text field (its decisions/0002), the owner compared three looks of the unchecked check beside the current
ring in `color.border.strong` at `stroke.regular`, on an iPhone 17 in light (catalog renders of a throwaway prototype
that changed no token, contract or WestronUI code):

- A: a disc filled with `color.background.muted`, no ring;
- B: a ring in `color.border.default` at `stroke.regular`, no fill;
- C: a ring in `color.border.strong` at `stroke.thin`.

## The owner's decision

**B: unchecked, the check is a quiet ring, `color.border.default` at `stroke.regular`,** the same for every list. The
checked state is unchanged: the disc in the list's color, or the accent, with its mark; so are the motions, the press,
the size, the hit area, the focus ring (the system's), disabled, the behavior, the public API and the accessibility.

## Awaiting the owner's confirmation

- **The deviation covers the ring.** The owner accepted on 2026-11-20 that the text field's fill misses WCAG 1.4.11's
  3:1 for a component's boundary in the standard themes (text field decisions/0002). The quiet ring misses it too, so
  the coordinator took the same acceptance for it. Measured by `tools/westron tokens contrast`:

  | `border.default` against | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | `background.canvas` | 1.27 | 1.46 |
  | `background.surface` | 1.33 | 1.34 |
  | `background.sunken` | 1.20 | 1.53 |

  The ring it replaces, `border.strong`, measured 3.50, 3.66 and 3.29 in light and 4.12, 3.79 and 4.34 in dark. What
  still sets an unchecked check apart: its place at the start of a task's row, its shape, and its hit area, which is
  unchanged; the checked state, the disc, meets 3:1 as before (decisions/0001).
- **High contrast keeps the strong ring.** In `light-hc` and `dark-hc` the ring is `color.border.strong` at
  `stroke.regular`, which meets 4.5:1 there (5.27 and 6.22 against the canvas). `border.default` would reach 3:1 there
  (3.19 and 3.45 against the canvas) but not the high-contrast non-text target, 4.5:1. A case has no contrast key, so
  the contract's description states it, as Button's does for its outline; the style reads `colorSchemeContrast`.

## Pairs

No declared contrast pair changes. `border.default` keeps its `separator` declaration, decorative in the standard
themes and 3:1 in high contrast, which holds; `border.strong` keeps its `non-text` declaration against the grounds and
the muted fill, which holds, and is still drawn in high contrast. The deviation is in what the check draws, not in a
token's declaration, so design-language/08 records it rather than decisions/0012.
